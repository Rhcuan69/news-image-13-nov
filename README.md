# The World in One Day — 13 November 2026

Entry for make.fun bounty **news-image-01**: sum up 13 November 2026's world
news in one image.

`main.py` copies the pre-rendered front page `news.png` (1024×1024, 127 KB)
to `/output/entry.png`. Deterministic, no network, Python stdlib only.
`tools/render_news.py` draws the front page with Pillow from the day's
verified headlines (Reuters/AP/IEA/PAHO briefings of 13 Nov 2026); claims
that could not be independently verified are labelled as such in the image.

Lead story: tankers hit in the Strait of Hormuz. Also covered: Europe joins
the next Ukraine peace talks, Nvidia chief Jensen Huang resigns, the
Microsoft 365 outage, the Senate leadership fight, the Brooklyn collapse,
and the U.S. move to seize $1.2bn from the Binance hack.
